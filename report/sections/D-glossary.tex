\chapter{Glossary}
\label{app:glossary}

The terms that recur from chapter to chapter.

\begin{description}[style=unboxed,leftmargin=0pt,labelindent=0pt,labelsep=0.4em,
                    itemsep=2.4pt,parsep=0pt]

\item[Adapter.] The small set of extra, trainable numbers that a LoRA fine-tune
  adds beside a frozen model. Chapter~\ref{ch:groups} trains four, one per
  behavioural group.

\item[Baseline (persistence).] The rule ``predict that the next \cid{cell\_id}
  is the last one seen''. No model, no training, and not a constant: 69.2\,\% on
  a raw day, 43.1\,\% on mobile users, because the population differs.

\item[Behavioural group, or profile.] One of four clusters of users, cut on the
  rate at which their \emph{physical site} changes over four bands of the day and
  named by increasing mobility: G0 homebody, G1 steady, G2 evening mover, G3
  morning mover.

\item[\cid{cell\_id}, site root, sector.] The identifier of one radio cell, e.g.\
  \cid{UKVCHE102}: a technology letter (2G or 3G), a \emph{site root} naming the
  physical antenna, \cid{KVCHE} here, then its \emph{sector}. Mobility is measured
  on the site root, since 12.4\,\% of consecutive pairs change cell without
  changing place; the target stays the full \cid{cell\_id}.

\item[Context fraction, context window.] The \emph{fraction} is the share of a
  user's day given to the model before it must predict the rest, 80\,\% by
  default, and it belongs to the measurement: moving it moves the baseline too.
  The \emph{window} is the architectural limit on how many tokens the model may
  look at in one go, 512 here.

\item[Gap.] Model accuracy minus persistence-baseline accuracy, in points, on
  the same users and the same positions. The only quantity here that is
  comparable across datasets.

\item[Hour token.] A symbol \cid{<H00>} to \cid{<H23>} placed before each event,
  giving its hour of day. Always given to the model, never predicted by it.

\item[LoRA, QLoRA.] Fine-tuning methods that freeze the original weights and
  train a small set of extra numbers beside them. QLoRA additionally stores the
  frozen weights in 4-bit precision, which quarters the memory they occupy.

\item[Paired bootstrap.] A significance test in which the same test users are
  resampled for both models compared, so that the luck of the draw cancels. An
  interval containing zero means the two cannot be told apart.

\item[Protocol: last fifth, or full sequence.] ``Last 20\,\%'' scores only the
  final fifth of a test user's day; ``full sequence'' scores every position, one
  at a time, which is harder and lowers the baseline on the same users from
  45.5\,\% to 43.1\,\%.

\item[Record, or event.] One (\cid{cell\_id}, timestamp) pair. Not a movement: an
  idle handset still produces records, because the network re-signals to it on a
  fixed cycle.

\item[Top-1, top-3, top-5.] Whether the correct cell is the model's top
  candidate, or among its top three or five of the 369 in the vocabulary.

\end{description}
