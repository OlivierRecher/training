\chapter{Reproducibility}
\label{app:repro}

This appendix records what would be needed to reproduce the results. Three
tables cover it: the training configuration of every run family, the artefact
behind every headline number quoted in the body, and the handful of
configuration keys that encode a decision rather than a preference. The
datasets themselves are described in Table~\ref{tab:datasets}, with counts
measured on the files rather than quoted.
