\chapter{Reproducibility}
\label{app:repro}

Three tables record what would be needed to reproduce the results: the training
settings of every family of runs, the run behind every number quoted in the body,
and the configuration keys that encode a decision argued somewhere in the report
rather than a preference. \textbf{Table~\ref{tab:hyperparams}} gives the settings,
\textbf{Table~\ref{tab:provenance}} the origin of every number quoted in the body, and
\textbf{Table~\ref{tab:config-keys}} the eleven keys that encode an argued decision. The
sets of people themselves are described in \textbf{Table~\ref{tab:datasets}}.
