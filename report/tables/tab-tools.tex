\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textit{Base models}}\\
Mistral-7B-v0.1 & 7B general-purpose model & First real run & \textbf{Abandoned}, $\approx$5.5\,h for a best validation of 41.1\,\% and no gain \\
Qwen2.5-0.5B-In\-struct & 0.5B model, $14\times$ smaller & Every run after the second iteration & \textbf{Kept}, $\approx$29 min, and the task needs no world knowledge \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textit{Representation}}\\
Dedicated cell tokens & One \cid{cell\_id} = one token & 369 added to the vocabulary & \textbf{Kept}, turns the prediction into an argmax over the cells \\
Dedicated hour tokens & One hour = one token & \cid{<H00>}\dots\cid{<H23>}, one per event & \textbf{Kept}, 1 token per event leaves the context window for history \\
Behavioural group token & Condition the whole sequence & Prepended, then replaced by adapter routing & \textbf{Kept, but only once routed} (Chapter~\ref{ch:groups}) \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textit{Training discipline}}\\
Weighted loss & Make some positions matter more & Transition-window targets & \textbf{Kept}, works, but only where the window is right \\
Weighted sampler ($\times3$) & Draw hard windows more often & Windows containing a transition & \textbf{Kept, with a cost}, wins in-window, loses outside \\
Gradient accumulation & Fit an effective batch on a T4 & All runs & \textbf{Kept} \\
Early stopping + best checkpoint & Keep the peak, not the last epoch & All runs & \textbf{Kept}, the single largest one-step gain of the internship \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textit{Compute and analysis}}\\
Google Colab (T4 / L4) & Cloud GPU & Every reported run & \textbf{Kept}, under a daily free quota \\
pandas, scikit-learn & Sampling and the stratified user split & Split stratified on record count & \textbf{Kept} \\
scipy & Length-balance check between train and test & Mann--Whitney test on the split & \textbf{Kept} \\
Paired bootstrap & Is a difference real? & 2{,}000 resamples over the test users & \textbf{Kept}, decisive in Chapter~\ref{ch:scale} \\
