\figpara{What was done, and what should have happened.}{One trained model, one set
of people, scored twice: once with a short slice of each person's day as history,
30 to 60\,\% of it, and once with a long one, 80 to 90\,\%. The \textbf{Change}

not merely decline, it falls to zero or nearly, and \emph{which} moments a long

correctly through the morning and then scores nothing on their evening is not
short of capacity. It is confidently repeating the last antenna it saw, with up to
78\,\% confidence in several of these cases, at exactly the moment that person
stops staying put. \smob, one trained model evaluated at several cuts.}}
