\vspace{3pt}
\begin{minipage}{\textwidth}\footnotesize
The fitted scaling law says the same thing: a slope of $+0.18\pt$ per ten-fold
increase in users, with $R^2 = 0.12$. At that slope one extra point would
require about 1.5 billion users --- the reductio that shows the curve is flat
rather than merely slow. The correct statement is not ``the gain is small'' but
``no gain was measured''.
\end{minipage}
