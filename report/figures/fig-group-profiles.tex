\caption[Hourly site-change profile of the four behavioural groups]{What a
behavioural group is: a shape in time, not a level. For every pair of
back-to-back records the question is whether the \emph{site root} changed;
the bar is the share of pairs that did, hour by hour. G0 is flat and low all
day; G1 moves at a constant moderate rate; G2 has a calm morning then climbs to
an 84\,\% peak at 19h; G3 peaks at 79\,\% before noon then settles for the
evening --- G2 and G3 are near mirror images. Percentages are shares of users.
\smob, 400 training users. The four labelled peaks are the values printed on
the source chart; the remaining bars are recovered from its shape geometry and
carry about $\pm1\pt$.}
