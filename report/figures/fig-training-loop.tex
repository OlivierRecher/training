\caption{How one training run works}
\label{fig:training-loop}
\figtext{%
\figpara{Steps 1 to 4 are the whole of training.}{A slice of somebody's day is
shown to the model with the next antenna hidden. The model answers, the answer is
compared with the truth and turned into a single number measuring how surprised
the model was, and that number nudges the model's adjustable numbers in the
direction that would have made it less wrong. Only a small part ever moves: the
500 million numbers the model was published with are frozen, and the nudging
applies to about 2 in every 100 added beside them.}

\figpara{Step 5 is not an optimisation, it is a safety rule.}{Left to itself, a
model trained on a few hundred short days stops learning the habits of the
population and starts learning the days by heart, while the training figures
happily report success for another forty passes. Grading on separate people after
each pass, and keeping the best copy rather than the last, was the largest single
improvement of the whole internship.}}
