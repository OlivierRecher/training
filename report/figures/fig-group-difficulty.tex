\caption{The kind of user predicts difficulty, and can be read from the past}
\label{fig:group-difficulty}
\figtext{%
\figpara{Panel (a): the label says in advance how hard somebody will be.}{Two
bars per kind of user, and they are not on a common scale, so read each one
against its own neighbours rather than against the other colour. The orange bar
is how often that kind of user changes location over the day. The grey-blue bar
is what the one-line rule scores on them.}

\figpara{And the two rankings are exact opposites.}{The stay-at-home user changes
location about a third of the time, and ``repeat the last antenna'' is already
right 56.3\,\% of the time for them. The morning-moving user changes location half
again as often, and the same rule is right only 17.8\,\% of the time. Moving from
left to right, the orange bars rise and the grey-blue bars fall, with no
exception. That is what one would hope for, and it is worth verifying rather than
assuming, because it is what makes the label useful before any model runs.}

\figpara{Panel (b): the constraint that makes the whole idea usable.}{At the
moment a prediction is asked for, only the person's past exists. So the kind of
user has to be worked out from that past alone, never from the part being
predicted, or the whole exercise is circular. These bars measure how often the
kind identified from the past alone matches the kind computed from the complete
day.}

\figpara{It works, but only with most of a day in hand.}{Given 80\,\% of the day,
the pipeline identifies the right kind for 92 of the 100 held-out people, unaided,
and 90\,\% gives the same figure. Below roughly 60\,\% it collapses: 61\,\% at
half a day, 44\,\% at 30\,\%. There is simply not enough history to tell a
morning mover from an evening mover.}

\figpara{Which is an uncomfortable place for the limit to fall.}{The mechanism is
worth using on somebody with most of a day behind them, and worth nothing on
somebody who has just appeared. That is exactly the case in which an operator has
the least information and would most want a prediction, and
Chapter~\ref{ch:conclusion} returns to it. \smob, 400 training and 100 held-out
people.}}
