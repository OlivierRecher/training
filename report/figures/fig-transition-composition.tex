\caption[Composition of consecutive-event pairs]{What a pair of back-to-back
records actually is. On \sraw, 43.6\,\% of pairs repeat
the same \cid{cell\_id} outright and a further 12.4\,\% change cell without
changing place; only 44.0\,\% are a real site change. Restricting the corpus to
mobile, non-absent users (\smob) moves the mass towards real movement ---
51\,\% --- while the middle band stays at 12\,\%. That middle band is why every
mobility measurement in this report is taken on the site root.}
