\caption{What one prediction is, and where a model can gain}
\label{fig:worked-example}
\figtext{%
The task, on two positions of one day. Everything to the left of the dashed line
is history the model reads, one hour token and one \cid{cell\_id} per event; the
boxed position is the one being scored, and the model is told its hour but not
its cell. The two panels deliberately share the same history: what differs is
the moment being predicted. In (a) the user has not moved, so answering ``the
last cell again'' is correct and both predictors score the point for nothing; in
(b) the user leaves, the trivial rule necessarily fails, and the answer has to
come from somewhere else. The two cases are illustrations, with identifiers taken
from the study area's own vocabulary; the measured share of each kind is in
Chapter~\ref{ch:data}.}
