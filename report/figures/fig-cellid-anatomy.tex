\figtext{%
\figpara{The name is not a code, it is a description.}{On the left, the antenna
name \cid{BSOCHO1} taken apart: the first letter is the generation of radio, the
middle letters name the mast, the last digits say which antenna of that mast
served the phone. Only the middle part corresponds to a place on a map. On the
right, one mast in the town of Cheb appears under four names. On the day used for
Chapter~\ref{ch:groups}, \textbf{12.4 out of every 100} back-to-back records are
exactly that: a new name, the same mast, a person who did not move. So
\emph{movement} is always measured on the mast, while what has to be
\emph{predicted} stays the full antenna name.}}
