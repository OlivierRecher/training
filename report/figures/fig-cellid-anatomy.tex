   used for the behavioural analysis, \textbf{12.4\,\%} of all
   consecutive-event pairs are ``same site root, different \cid{cell\_id}'':
   the handset re-attached to another cell of the same antenna while the person
   did not go anywhere. Counting that as movement would blur the very
   distinction the behavioural profiles of Chapter~\ref{ch:groups} exist to
   capture, so mobility is always computed on the site root, never on the raw
   \cid{cell\_id}.};
