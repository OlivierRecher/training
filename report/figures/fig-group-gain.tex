           \swatch{clmodel}~model, group routed to its own adapter}

\begin{tikzpicture}

  symbolic x coords={G0,G1,G2,G3,MEAN}, xtick=data,

\end{axis}

\caption[Per-group gain after routing the group to its own adapter]{The gain
ranks in exactly the reverse order of the baseline: G0 is already solved by
repetition and has almost nothing left to win, while G3 --- where repetition is
right less than one time in five --- gains $+6.4\pt$. The correlation between
how easy a group is and how much the model adds to it is $-0.99$ across the
four groups. Weighted by how many users each group actually holds, the overall
gain is $+3.3\pt$ rather than the $+3.7\pt$ of the unweighted mean, because
28\,\% of the population sits in the group that needs no model. \smob,
100 held-out users, full sequence.}
\label{fig:group-gain}
\end{figure}
