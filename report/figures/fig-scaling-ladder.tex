\caption[The whole scaling ladder, in points over each run's own baseline]{Eight
training-set sizes, each read against the persistence baseline measured on its
\emph{own} test users --- the only quantity that survives a change of test set.
The 500 to 2{,}000-user tiers are scored on \stwok (100 test users, 48.2\,\%
baseline; the 500-user point is the \smob run, 43.1\,\% baseline), the
5{,}000 to 21{,}000-user tiers on \seleven (1{,}000 test users, 50.2\,\%
baseline). A four-fold increase from 500 to 2{,}000 users buys $+3.6\pt$; the
ten-fold increase that follows buys $+0.1\pt$, and none of the steps inside it
is statistically distinguishable from zero (Table~\ref{tab:bootstrap}). The
curve does not slow down --- it stops.}
