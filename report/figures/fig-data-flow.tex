\figpara{Figure~\ref{fig:data-flow}: data preprocessing pipeline.}{At the top of Figure~\ref{fig:data-flow} is one person's whole day

antenna and the time in seconds since midnight, over and over. \textbf{(1)} Filter non-trajectory records. \textbf{(2)} Prepend explicit hourly tokens. \textbf{(3)} Construct single-subscriber sequence lines. \textbf{(4)} Partition subscribers into disjoint training and held-out evaluation sets.
