\caption[Accuracy by cell-switch rate]{Accuracy is set by how repetitive the
user already is, and the model's contribution runs the other way. (a)~On the
unfiltered day, users who never change cell are predicted perfectly by both the
baseline and the model, and the most volatile fifth plateaus at 34.4\,\%; the
model only overtakes the baseline in the two hardest groups, which together
hold 18.5\,\% of the predictions. (b)~The same ordering on the 100 held-out
mobile users of \smob: the model \emph{loses} to the baseline everywhere
except the most volatile quartile. Baselines are recomputed per user, on the
same positions the model is scored on.}
